  - zonal, zone-redundant and non-regional services;

  - redundancy (LRS, ZRS, GRS, GZRS and the RA- variants), durability nines, and RPO under 15 minutes;

  - ExpressRoute not being encrypted by default, and what still crosses the internet.
- Identity:
  - Entra Domain Services' one-way sync;
  - passwordless methods;
  - B2B collaboration versus B2B direct connect.
- Governance:
