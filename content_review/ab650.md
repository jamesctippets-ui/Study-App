## Patterns

This track's content is a mix of an older core (cards f1 to f130, questions q1 to q83) and a newer layer written against the study guide (ids f90xx, q90xx), and the newer layer is accurate and well keyed. The problems were almost all currency: the product moves quickly, so several items still described Semi-Annual Enterprise Channel as a twice-a-year channel (unified with Monthly Enterprise Channel since Version 2606 in July 2026), group-based licensing as skipping users without a usage location (they inherit the tenant location), "Power Platform DLP policies" (now data policies), "Graph connectors" (now Copilot connectors) and an "Agents area that grew out of Integrated apps". The second main problem was blanking: about 20 definitions repeated the term (often because the front carried extra words such as a prefix, so the app did not blank it), four fronts named two concepts so two different terms were blanked identically or an acronym was half visible (f4, f34, f81, f9032), and several true/false items restated a multiple-choice question. Distractors were mostly sound; a handful were absurd (q14, q44) and about ten correct options were conspicuously the longest. Overall quality is good once the dated items are fixed; the remaining risk is the preview-era detail in the newest cards listed above.
